# 8-Day Galapagos Cruise Aboard the M/Y Evolution

## Overview

Sail the enchanted Galapagos Islands aboard the elegant M/Y Evolution, a
classic 1930s-style motor yacht refitted for modern comfort. Over eight
unforgettable days you'll snorkel with sea lions, walk among giant tortoises,
and witness the islands that inspired Darwin's theory of evolution. This
small-ship voyage carries just 32 guests, ensuring an intimate, expert-led
experience.

## Cruise Highlights

- Snorkel alongside playful sea lions and marine iguanas
- Walk among giant tortoises in the Santa Cruz highlands
- Spot blue-footed boobies and waved albatrosses on Española
- Expert naturalist guides certified by the Galapagos National Park
- All-inclusive comfort aboard a 32-guest classic yacht

## Itinerary

1. Day 1 — Arrive in Baltra, transfer to the yacht, afternoon at Las Bachas Beach
2. Day 2 — Genovesa Island: Darwin Bay and Prince Philip's Steps
3. Day 3 — Santiago Island: Sullivan Bay lava fields and Bartolome's pinnacle
4. Day 4 — Santa Cruz: Charles Darwin Station and tortoise highlands
5. Day 5 — Floreana: Post Office Bay and Devil's Crown snorkeling
6. Day 6 — Española: Gardner Bay and Punta Suarez wildlife colonies
7. Day 7 — San Cristobal: Kicker Rock and the interpretation center
8. Day 8 — Disembark in San Cristobal, transfer to the airport

## The Ship

The M/Y Evolution blends vintage elegance with contemporary amenities. She
features a sun deck with a jacuzzi, an indoor lounge and bar, and a dining room
serving international and Ecuadorian cuisine. Stabilizers ensure smooth sailing
between islands.

## Cabins & Accommodation

All 16 cabins offer ocean views, private bathrooms, climate control, and ample
storage. Choose from Premium, Superior, and Suite categories across the Main

## FAQ

### Is the Galapagos cruise suitable for non-swimmers?

Yes. While snorkeling is a highlight, every excursion offers dry-landing and
walking alternatives, and flotation aids are available for all water activities.

### What is the best time of year for a Galapagos cruise?

The Galapagos is a year-round destination. The warm season (December–May)
brings calmer seas and clear water, while the cool season (June–November) offers
more active wildlife and seabird breeding.
