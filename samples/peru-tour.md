## FAQ

### Is altitude a concern in Cusco?

Cusco sits at 3,400 m (11,150 ft). The itinerary builds in acclimatization
time, and most travelers adjust within a day or two with rest and hydration.

### How far in advance should I book?

Machu Picchu permits and Inca Trail spaces sell out months ahead, especially
for the June–August high season. We recommend booking 3–6 months in advance.
