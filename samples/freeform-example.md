## A Voyage Built Around Wildlife

We time our landings to the rhythms of the islands — sunrise seabird colonies,
midday snorkeling with sea lions, and golden-hour walks among giant tortoises.

Our naturalists adjust each day to where the wildlife is most active, so no two
sailings are ever quite the same.
